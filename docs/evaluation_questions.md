# Evaluation Questions

Source documents:
- **SOLO 1 / SOLO 2** — Simpson et al., *N Engl J Med* 2016;375:2335-48 (NEJM)
- **JAAD OLE** — Deleuran et al., *J Am Acad Dermatol* 2020;82:377-88, open-label extension study (NCT01949311)
- **Label** — DUPIXENT (dupilumab) FDA Prescribing Information (761055s075lbl.pdf)

---

## Questions

1. What is the mechanism of action of dupilumab as described in the prescribing information?
2. What was the design of SOLO 1, including randomization ratio, blinding, and treatment duration?
3. How many patients were enrolled in SOLO 1 and how many in SOLO 2?
4. What was the primary endpoint of SOLO 1 and SOLO 2?
5. What proportion of patients achieved the primary outcome in SOLO 1 with dupilumab every other week versus placebo?
6. What was the design of the open-label extension study (NCT01949311) reported in the JAAD article, and how does it differ from SOLO 1/SOLO 2 in terms of blinding and control arm?
7. How many patients were enrolled in the open-label extension study, and how many patient-years of exposure did this represent?
8. What were the most common adverse events reported in the open-label extension study, and at what rate (events/100 patient-years)?
9. According to the prescribing information, what adverse reactions occur in ≥1% of patients receiving DUPIXENT monotherapy for atopic dermatitis, and how do their rates compare to placebo?
10. What was the long-term open-label extension trial referenced in the DUPIXENT label for adults with atopic dermatitis, and through how many weeks of treatment was safety assessed?
11. How did the incidence of conjunctivitis compare between dupilumab and placebo groups in SOLO 1 and SOLO 2?
12. What was the EASI-75 response rate at week 16 in SOLO 1 and SOLO 2 for patients receiving weekly dupilumab, and how do these compare to placebo?
13. How did EASI scores change over time in the open-label extension study, and how did the trajectory compare between the dupilumab-naive and retreatment subgroups?
14. What were the two deaths reported in the SOLO trials, and in which specific trial did they occur?
15. What were the efficacy findings that were consistent between SOLO 1 and SOLO 2 at week 16?
16. How does the safety and efficacy profile of dupilumab reported in the JAAD open-label extension study (up to 76 weeks) compare with the 16-week findings from SOLO 1 and SOLO 2?
17. How does the exposure duration and patient population of the AD-1225 open-label extension trial in the DUPIXENT label compare with the open-label extension study (NCT01949311) reported in the JAAD article?
18. What dosing regimen for adults with atopic dermatitis is specified in the current DUPIXENT prescribing information, and how does this compare with the weekly 300 mg regimen used in the JAAD open-label extension study?
19. What was the rate of study drug discontinuation due to adverse events in SOLO 1/SOLO 2 (through week 16) compared to the open-label extension study (through 76 weeks)?
20. According to the DUPIXENT label's summary of the SOLO 1, SOLO 2, and CHRONOS trials, how did the IGA 0/1 response rate in CHRONOS (with concomitant topical corticosteroids) compare to the monotherapy trials SOLO 1 and SOLO 2?
